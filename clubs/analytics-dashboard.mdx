The TPC analytics dashboard gives you a live view of how your club profile performs — how many people see it, how many click through, and how many convert into enquiries. Beyond raw traffic, the dashboard includes a fee benchmarking tool that shows how your initiation fee and annual dues compare to similar clubs in your area. You access everything from a single screen inside the club management portal; no separate logins or third-party tools required.

## Real-Time Metrics

The dashboard updates continuously. Every metric below reflects current data, not yesterday's export.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Views" icon="eye">
    The number of times a prospect opened your full club profile page. A view means someone clicked through from search results or an AI answer and spent time reading your listing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Impressions" icon="magnifying-glass">
    The number of times your club appeared in a search result or AI response — whether or not the prospect clicked through. High impressions with low views signal a title or thumbnail that could be stronger.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Enquiries" icon="envelope">
    The number of pre-qualified leads delivered to you from your profile. Track this number against views to understand your profile-to-enquiry conversion rate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lead Sources" icon="arrow-pointer">
    A breakdown of where your enquiries originated — TPC directory search, AI assistant referral, direct link, or the director community referral network.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Fee Benchmarking

The benchmarking tool compares your club's initiation fee and annual dues to verified clubs of a similar type within your geographic market. Use it to answer the questions that come up in every board meeting:

- Are we priced competitively relative to peers in our metro area?
- Has the local market moved since we last reviewed our fee schedule?
- Are our dues above or below the median for clubs with comparable amenities?

<Note>
  Fee benchmark data is drawn exclusively from verified clubs that have published their fee schedules on TPC. The more clubs in your market claim their listings, the more precise the benchmark becomes. Encourage peer clubs to claim — it improves data quality for everyone.
</Note>

## Lead Source Breakdown

Understanding where enquiries come from helps you prioritise where to invest your profile's attention. The lead source panel shows:

<Accordion title="TPC Directory Search">
  Leads generated when a prospect searched TPC's directory and selected your club from the results. A high share here indicates your profile ranks well for relevant search filters — location, membership type, amenities, and fee range.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="AI Assistant Referral">
  Leads generated when an AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, or Perplexity) cited your profile in an answer and the prospect followed the link. This channel grows as AI assistant usage increases — it requires no action from you beyond maintaining an accurate verified profile.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Direct Link">
  Leads from prospects who arrived at your profile via a shared URL — for example, a link in your own marketing materials, social media, or a referral from a current member.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Director Community Referral">
  Leads passed to you by fellow membership directors in the TPC community — for example, when a club in another market receives an enquiry from a prospect moving to your area and refers them on. See [Director Community](/clubs/director-community) for more on how the referral network works.
</Accordion>

## Using the Dashboard to Optimise Your Profile

Your metrics tell you exactly where to focus improvement efforts:

<Steps>

  <Step title="Check Your Impression-to-View Ratio">
    If impressions are high but views are low, your club is appearing in searches but not getting the click. Review your profile's headline photo and the first two lines of your about section — these are the only elements visible in search result cards.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check Your View-to-Enquiry Ratio">
    If views are high but enquiries are low, prospects are reading your profile but not converting. Common causes: outdated waitlist status (showing closed when you have capacity), a fee that appears out of step with the market, or a gallery with too few photos. Use the benchmarking tool to rule out fee misalignment first.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review Your Fee Benchmark Position">
    Open the benchmarking panel and compare your initiation fee and dues to the local median. If you are significantly above the median, ensure your profile narrative articulates the premium clearly. If you are below the median, consider whether you are leaving money on the table.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Monitor AI Referral Share Over Time">
    AI assistant referral should grow as a share of your lead sources month over month. If it stagnates, it may indicate your profile data is incomplete — AI assistants favour structured, detailed profiles. See [AI Discovery](/clubs/ai-discovery) for the full picture.
  </Step>

</Steps>

<Tip>
  Export a monthly metrics snapshot and share it with your general manager or board. The combination of impressions, views, and fee benchmark data is compelling evidence for membership marketing decisions.
</Tip>

## Accessing the Dashboard

Log in to your club management portal at [theprivateclubs.com](https://theprivateclubs.com) and navigate to **Analytics** in the left sidebar. The dashboard is available to all claimed clubs from the moment their verified profile goes live.
