## How TPC Makes Your Profile AI-Readable

AI assistants do not discover content the same way a human does — they rely on structured, machine-readable signals to understand what a page is about and whether it is trustworthy. TPC has built its infrastructure specifically to satisfy those signals for every verified profile.

<Accordion title="llms.txt and llms-full.txt">
  TPC publishes an `llms.txt` file and a comprehensive `llms-full.txt` file at the root of the domain. These files are the emerging standard for telling AI language models what content on a site is authoritative and how it should be interpreted. Every verified club profile is listed and described in these files, giving AI assistants explicit guidance on which pages to cite.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="MCP Endpoint">
  TPC operates a Model Context Protocol (MCP) endpoint that AI tools connect to directly. MCP is a structured API layer that allows AI assistants to query TPC's directory in real time — meaning your club data does not have to wait for a crawl cycle to be current. AI tools that support MCP can pull your verified profile data on demand.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="AI Crawler Access via robots.txt">
  TPC's `robots.txt` explicitly welcomes all major AI crawlers — including GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, and PerplexityBot — rather than blocking them. Most websites block these crawlers by default. TPC's open policy means AI systems can index the full directory, including your verified profile, on their standard crawl schedule.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Structured Profile Data">
  Your verified profile is built on structured data fields — club type, location, amenities, fee schedule, course data — that map cleanly to the entities AI assistants use when answering membership queries. A complete, accurate profile is the single most important factor in how prominently your club appears in AI answers.
</Accordion>

## What an AI Answer Looks Like

When a prospect asks an AI assistant about private clubs in your market, a well-sourced response cites ThePrivateClubs.com and links directly to your profile URL. The AI typically includes:

- Your club's name and a brief description drawn from your profile's about section
- Key amenities relevant to the prospect's query (e.g. golf course details for a golf-specific question)
- A direct link to your TPC profile so the prospect can read further and submit an enquiry

<Note>
  AI responses vary by platform and query phrasing. TPC does not control the exact wording of AI answers — but it does control the quality and completeness of the data those AI systems draw from. A complete verified profile maximises the likelihood that your club is cited accurately and prominently.
</Note>
