Every lead that reaches you through The Private Clubs comes from someone who is actively searching for a private club membership — not someone who saw an ad and clicked out of curiosity. TPC pre-filters prospects by location, membership type, and budget before a single enquiry leaves the platform, so your pipeline stays focused on people who can actually join. You choose where those leads land: your inbox, your phone, your WhatsApp, or directly into your CRM.

## How Leads Are Qualified

TPC collects structured information from every prospect at the point of enquiry. Before a lead is delivered to you, it is filtered against three criteria:

- **Location match** — the prospect's current location and intended relocation area align with your club's market
- **Membership type** — the prospect's stated interests (golf, social, family, fitness, etc.) match your club's offering
- **Budget alignment** — the prospect's net worth range and stated willingness to pay cover your initiation fee and dues

Leads that do not meet all three criteria are not routed to your club. This keeps your enquiry queue clean and your conversion rate high.

## The Lead Card Format

Every lead arrives as a structured card containing the following fields:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Name" icon="user">
    The prospect's full name as submitted during their TPC membership search.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Location" icon="location-dot">
    Current city and state, plus any stated relocation destination.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Net Worth Range" icon="dollar-sign">
    A banded estimate (e.g. $25M–$50M) drawn from the prospect's self-reported profile.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Timeline" icon="clock">
    How soon the prospect intends to join — for example, within 30, 60, or 90 days.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Focus & Interests" icon="star">
    The prospect's stated priorities, such as golf, family amenities, social dining, fitness, or a combination.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Direct Contact Details" icon="address-card">
    Email address and phone number so you can follow up immediately without going through a third-party platform.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Delivery Channels

TPC routes leads to wherever you actually work. Configure your delivery preferences in the club management portal and update them at any time.

<Accordion title="Email">
  Lead cards are sent to your nominated email address — typically in a `leads@theprivateclubs.com` forwarding format — with the full structured card in the message body. Ideal if you manage your pipeline from a desktop inbox or forward leads to a team.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="SMS">
  A concise lead summary is sent as a text message to your mobile number the moment a new enquiry matches your club. Use SMS if you are on the floor and need an instant heads-up before checking full details on the dashboard.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="WhatsApp">
  The complete lead card — all fields, including direct contact details — is delivered to your WhatsApp. Useful for membership directors who already communicate with prospects and colleagues via WhatsApp.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="CRM Integration">
  TPC can push lead data directly into your existing membership CRM so enquiries appear alongside your current pipeline without any manual data entry. Contact the TPC team to configure your CRM connection during onboarding.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Use multiple channels in combination. For example, set SMS for instant notification and CRM for structured record-keeping. Your delivery preferences do not have to be exclusive.
</Tip>

## The Unified Enquiries Dashboard

Regardless of how leads are delivered, every enquiry is also collected in your TPC club management portal under the **Enquiries** tab. The dashboard gives you:

- A chronological list of all leads received
- Status tracking (new, contacted, in progress, joined, declined)
- The full lead card for every enquiry in one place
- A searchable history you can filter by date, lead source, or status

<Warning>
  Do not rely solely on delivery channel notifications. Occasionally, SMS or email delivery can be delayed by carrier filters. Always treat the dashboard as the authoritative record of your enquiries.
</Warning>
