Claiming your listing on The Private Clubs is the fastest way to put your club in front of high-net-worth prospects who are actively searching for membership — not just browsing. Verified clubs surface first in every search result, and your profile begins appearing in AI assistant answers the moment it goes live. The process takes minutes to start and less than 24 hours to complete.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Find Your Club in the Directory">
    Go to [theprivateclubs.com/clubs](https://theprivateclubs.com/clubs) and search for your club by name, city, or state. Every club in North America already has a base listing in the directory — you are claiming and upgrading an existing record, not creating one from scratch.

    <Tip>
      Search by partial name if your club's full legal name differs from its common name. If you cannot locate your listing, contact the TPC team directly and they will create your base record before you proceed.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply for a Founding Club Spot">
    Select your club's listing and click **Claim This Club**. Complete the short application form with your name, title, club name, and contact details. Founding Club pricing is locked for life and limited to the first 100 clubs — once those spots are filled, the rate increases.

    <Warning>
      Founding Club pricing is only available during the initial rollout. If you see the Founding Club badge is still available on your club's listing page, act now — spots are claimed on a first-come, first-served basis.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay the One-Time Claiming Fee">
    Complete the **$500 one-time payment** to activate your claim. This single fee covers everything needed to get your verified profile live:

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Identity Verification" icon="shield-check">
        TPC confirms you are an authorised representative of the club before publishing any changes.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Profile Production" icon="pen-nib">
        TPC's editorial team writes and produces your full club profile story — no copywriting required from you.
      </Card>
      <Card title="AI Placement" icon="robot">
        Your profile is structured and submitted so it surfaces in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and Perplexity answers.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Founding Club Badge" icon="award">
        Your listing displays the exclusive Founding Club badge, signalling heritage and priority status to prospects.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    After the one-time fee, your ongoing access to leads, analytics, and the director community is billed at **$99/month** (annual billing), locked at the Founding Club rate for as long as you remain a member.
  </Step>

  <Step title="TPC Builds Your Profile Within 24 Hours">
    Once payment is confirmed, the TPC editorial team takes over. They research your club, write the full profile narrative, populate amenities, fee structures, and upload your gallery. You do not need to write a single word.

    Expect a review link in your inbox within **24 hours**. You will have the opportunity to request any changes before the profile goes live.

    <Note>
      Have your club's current initiation fee, annual dues, and waitlist status ready to share. The faster TPC receives accurate figures, the sooner your profile goes live. See [Your Verified Profile](/clubs/verified-profile) for a full breakdown of what the profile includes.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Your Lead Delivery Preferences">
    Before your profile goes live, configure where you want pre-qualified enquiries delivered. TPC routes leads to wherever you actually work — choose one or combine multiple channels:

    - **Email** — leads arrive formatted and ready to action
    - **SMS** — instant notification to your mobile
    - **WhatsApp** — full lead details delivered to your WhatsApp
    - **CRM** — direct integration into your existing membership CRM

    See [Lead Management](/clubs/lead-management) for a full explanation of the lead format and delivery options.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go Live and Start Receiving Enquiries">
    Once you approve the profile, TPC publishes it. From that moment your club:

    - **Surfaces first** in directory search results ahead of unclaimed listings
    - **Appears in AI answers** when prospects ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, or Perplexity about clubs in your area
    - **Receives pre-qualified leads** filtered by location, membership type, and budget
    - **Gains access** to the analytics dashboard and the 2,400+ member director community

    <Tip>
      Check your [Analytics Dashboard](/clubs/analytics-dashboard) in the first week to see impressions and profile views ramp up. Use the fee benchmarking tool to confirm your initiation fee and dues are positioned competitively.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

</Steps>
