Your verified profile is more than a directory entry — it is the authoritative public record of your club on the largest private membership directory in North America. TPC's editorial team writes and produces the entire profile for you, ensuring it reads with the tone and credibility that high-net-worth prospects expect. Once live, verified profiles surface ahead of all unclaimed listings in search results and are the only profiles eligible to appear in AI assistant answers.

## What TPC Writes and Produces for You

TPC handles all profile production after you pay the one-time $500 claiming fee. You do not need to write copy, source photos from scratch, or format anything. The editorial team builds every section below.

<Accordion title="About Section">
  A professionally written narrative that tells your club's story — founding history, culture, membership philosophy, and what makes the experience distinct. TPC writes this in a tone appropriate for a discerning, high-net-worth audience. You review and approve before it goes live.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Amenities">
  A structured, scannable list of every facility and service your club offers — dining, fitness, tennis, pool, spa, marina, private events space, and more. Prospects filter search results by amenity, so a complete amenities section directly increases your discovery rate.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Initiation Fee and Annual Dues">
  Your current fee schedule is displayed clearly so prospects can self-qualify before they enquire. Accurate fee data also powers the [Analytics Dashboard](/clubs/analytics-dashboard) fee benchmarking tool, which lets you compare your pricing to similar clubs in your market.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Waitlist Status">
  A real-time indicator showing whether your club is open, has a waitlist, or is invite-only. This is one of the most-viewed fields on any profile — keep it current to avoid fielding enquiries your membership pipeline cannot accommodate.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Photos and Gallery">
  TPC populates your gallery with available photography. You can supply additional high-resolution images at any time through the club management portal. A rich gallery measurably increases profile dwell time and enquiry conversion.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Golf Course Data (Golf Clubs Only)">
  If your club operates a golf course or courses, TPC adds a dedicated course data section covering:

  - Number of holes per course
  - Par and total yardage
  - Course architect
  - Number of courses on property

  This data surfaces your listing when prospects specifically search for a course of a particular length, par, or pedigree.
</Accordion>

## You Control the Narrative

TPC writes the first version of every profile section, but you own the content. Log in to the club management portal at any time to:

- Update your initiation fee and annual dues
- Change your waitlist status
- Add or replace gallery images
- Edit your about section and amenities list
- Refresh course data after renovations

<Note>
  Keep your fee schedule and waitlist status current. Prospects who enquire based on outdated information create friction in your pipeline and reduce the quality score of your listing in TPC's ranking algorithm.
</Note>

## Why Verified Profiles Surface First

TPC's search algorithm gives verified profiles a permanent ranking advantage over unclaimed listings for two reasons:

1. **Data completeness** — verified profiles contain structured, accurate data across every field, which produces better matches for prospect search filters.
2. **Trust signals** — verified and Founding Club badges increase click-through rates, which TPC's algorithm interprets as relevance and rewards with higher placement.

The same completeness that drives search ranking also drives AI discovery. When ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, or Perplexity answer a question about private clubs in your area, they draw from TPC's structured profile data. An incomplete profile is invisible to AI answers; a complete verified profile is cited by name with a direct link. See [AI Discovery](/clubs/ai-discovery) for the full explanation.

</Tip>
