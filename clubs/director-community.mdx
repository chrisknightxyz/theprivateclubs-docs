If you run membership at a private club, this is the peer group you have been missing.

## What You Can Do in the Community

### Compare Fees With Peers Nationwide

Stop guessing where your initiation fee and dues sit relative to the market. The community gives you direct access to peers in comparable markets who are willing to share real numbers in a trusted, verified environment.

<Tip>
  Combine peer conversations in the community with the data from your [Analytics Dashboard](/clubs/analytics-dashboard) fee benchmarking tool. Qualitative context from a peer in your market is often more actionable than aggregate benchmark data alone.
</Tip>

### Share What Works

The community is built around honest, peer-to-peer exchange. Directors post what is working in their membership marketing — referral programmes, event formats, digital campaigns, onboarding experiences, waitlist management tactics — and what is not. You get unfiltered insight from people doing the same job, not consultant decks or conference presentations.

<Accordion title="Topics Directors Discuss">
  - New member acquisition strategies and conversion rates
  - Referral programme structures and incentives
  - Handling waitlists and managing prospect expectations
  - Seasonal membership and trial membership formats
  - How to use social media without compromising club exclusivity
  - Retention tactics for members in their first two years
  - How to present fee increases to existing membership
  - Using TPC analytics to support board-level reporting
</Accordion>

### Peer Referrals and Introductions

When a prospect contacts a club that is not the right fit — wrong location, wrong membership type, waitlist too long — the receiving director can refer that prospect to a peer in the community whose club is a better match. This referral network keeps high-quality prospects in the TPC ecosystem and builds goodwill between directors who may reciprocate in the future.

<Tip>
  Referrals work best when you have an established presence in the community. Introduce yourself, engage in a few discussions, and share your club's profile early. Directors refer prospects to people they know and trust.
</Tip>

## Get Started

<Steps>

  <Step title="Introduce Yourself">
    Post a brief introduction in the community's welcome thread — your club name, location, membership type, and what you are focused on this year. Other directors respond to new introductions; it is the fastest way to make your first connections.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Browse the Fee Comparison Threads">
    Filter community discussions by the **Fees & Benchmarking** tag to find conversations about initiation fees and dues in your region. Combine what you find with the data in your [Analytics Dashboard](/clubs/analytics-dashboard).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explore the Creator Roster">
    Navigate to the **Creators & Partners** section and filter by category and specialty. Save profiles of creators whose work fits your club's aesthetic for the next time you need content production support.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Referral Alerts">
    Enable notifications for prospects in your geographic market. When a director in the community flags a prospect who is looking for a club in your area, you will be notified immediately.
  </Step>

</Steps>
