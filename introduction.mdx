The Private Clubs is the largest directory of private membership clubs in North America, indexing more than 51,633 clubs across every US state and Canadian province. Whether you're searching for your next golf club, looking to join a city or yacht club, or managing membership enquiries for an established club, this platform gives you the data and tools to move efficiently.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Explore the Directory">
    Browse 51,633+ private clubs filtered by state, type, initiation fee, annual dues, and waitlist status — all in one place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare Your Shortlist">
    Put two or more clubs side by side to compare fees, amenities, waitlist timelines, and founding year.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply or Engage Concierge">
    Submit your application directly to the membership director, or engage the Concierge service for a dedicated advisor and warm introductions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Accepted">
    Track your application status and correspond with the club. The Concierge service includes full representation through acceptance.
  </Step>
</Steps>
