The Private Clubs member community is a private, verified network available exclusively to active members of private clubs. It is not open to the general public, not visible to non-members, and not accessible without confirmed club affiliation. The community exists so that people who share the private club lifestyle — across 3,898+ clubs and all 50 states — have a trusted place to connect, share experiences, ask questions, and build relationships that extend beyond the gates of any single club.

## Who Can Join the Community

Access to the member community is restricted to **active members of a private club** listed in The Private Clubs directory. Prospective members who have not yet joined a club are not eligible. This restriction is not arbitrary — it is what makes the community trustworthy. Every person you interact with has been through the same vetting process that membership in a private club requires.

<Note>
  If you are currently in the application or waitlist phase, you are not yet eligible for the community. Return to this page once your membership is confirmed and your verification is complete.
</Note>

## How Verification Works

The Private Clubs verifies your club affiliation before granting community access. Verification protects the privacy and integrity of the network for everyone in it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up for a Private Clubs account using your name and email address. Choose the membership plan that matches your access level (see plan details below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Your Affiliation Proof">
    Provide proof of your active club membership using one of three accepted methods:

    - **Member number** — your club-issued membership number
    - **Membership card photo** — a photo of your current membership card showing your name and club name
    - **Club confirmation** — direct confirmation from your club's membership office (TPC contacts the club on your behalf)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verification Review">
    The TPC verification team reviews your submission. Verification typically completes within **24–48 hours**. You receive an email confirmation once your account is approved and community access is activated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access the Community">
    After verification, log in to access the community features included in your plan — your own club's community, or the full nationwide network, depending on the tier you selected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Community Covers

The member community provides a private, moderated space for club members to:

- **Ask questions** about club life, amenities, and membership experience at specific clubs
- **Share recommendations** for clubs, courses, marinas, and member-favorite experiences
- **Discuss reciprocal privileges** and travel logistics across clubs nationwide
- **Connect with members** at clubs you are considering or have visited
- **Exchange local knowledge** about club culture, staff, and seasonal programming
- **Engage with club-specific groups** if you are on the All Clubs plan

## Privacy Protections

The community is entirely invisible to non-members and non-subscribers. The following privacy protections apply:

- **Non-members cannot see the community**, its members, or its content — not even with a free account
- **Your club affiliation is not exposed** to the general public or to other directory users outside the community
- **Member profiles within the community** display only the information you choose to share
- **Discussions are not indexed** by search engines or visible outside the platform

<Warning>
  Do not share club-specific information that your club's membership agreement designates as confidential. The community is private, but member conduct within it remains subject to the same discretion expected at the club itself.
</Warning>

## Coverage

The verified member community spans:

- **3,898+ clubs** with active member participants
- **All 50 states** represented
- All major club types — golf, country, yacht, city, athletic, aviation, equestrian, ski, and more

The community grows as new members complete verification. If your club has few existing participants, your presence helps establish it as a community hub for future members at your club.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What if my club is not in the directory?">
    The Private Clubs directory covers 51,633+ clubs. If your club is not listed, contact TPC support — clubs can be added to the directory, and once listed, you can complete verification against the new listing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch between the Your Club and All Clubs plans?">
    Yes. You can upgrade from Your Club to All Clubs at any time from your account settings. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my membership status changes?">
    If you leave your club or your membership lapses, your community access is suspended until you verify active membership at another club. Notify TPC support if your membership status changes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long does verification take if I use club confirmation?">
    Club confirmation takes slightly longer than the standard 24–48 hour window because it requires outreach to your club's membership office. Allow 2–4 business days for this method, depending on your club's response time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the community available on mobile?">
    Yes. The community is accessible through the mobile-responsive web platform. A dedicated mobile app is on the product roadmap — check the platform changelog for updates.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
