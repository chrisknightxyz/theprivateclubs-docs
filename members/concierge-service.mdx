Private club membership — especially at the most sought-after clubs — is not a transaction you complete online. It involves personal introductions, careful positioning, letters of recommendation, interviews, and board votes. The Membership Concierge service exists for members who want an expert in their corner for every step of that process. A dedicated TPC advisor manages your placement from initial matching through accepted membership, with a financial structure that ensures you pay nothing if the service doesn't deliver.

## How the Pricing Works

The Concierge service is structured so that you take on essentially zero financial risk.

### The $1,500 Deposit

You pay a **$1,500 one-time deposit** to engage the service. This deposit is:

- **Fully refundable** if your advisor does not secure a placement within 90 days
- **Credited in full against your initiation fee** upon acceptance at a club

In practice, the deposit costs you nothing if the service works — and costs you nothing if it doesn't.

<Note>
  The average initiation fee for a private golf club in the United States is approximately $60,000. The $1,500 Concierge deposit represents roughly **2.5% of that average initiation fee** — and it comes back to you either as a credit at the club or as a refund. There is no net cost.
</Note>

### The 90-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If your dedicated advisor does not secure a placement offer within 90 days of engagement, The Private Clubs refunds your $1,500 deposit in full. No questions, no partial credit — a complete refund.

<Tip>
  The 90-day window is a service-level commitment, not a deadline. If you are pursuing a multi-year waitlist club and a placement is in process, your advisor will discuss expectations with you transparently from the start.
</Tip>

## The Concierge Process Step by Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Engage the Service">
    Submit your $1,500 deposit through the Concierge enrollment page. You will also complete a brief intake form covering your target location, club type preferences, budget range, timeline, and any specific clubs already on your radar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Advisor Assigned Within 24 Hours">
    A dedicated TPC membership advisor is assigned to your account within 24 hours of enrollment. Your advisor reviews your intake form personally — not an algorithm — and prepares for your initial consultation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initial Consultation">
    Your advisor contacts you to discuss your preferences in depth, clarify priorities, and explain the clubs they are already considering for you. This conversation shapes the matching process that follows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="3–5 Clubs Identified and Presented">
    Your advisor presents a curated set of 3–5 clubs that match your criteria, with a brief rationale for each recommendation. You review the options, ask questions, and together with your advisor select the clubs you want to pursue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Warm Introductions Made">
    Your advisor reaches out to each selected club's membership director with a personal introduction on your behalf. These are relationship-driven contacts, not form submissions — the membership director knows your advisor and takes the introduction seriously.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Profile Delivered to Each Club">
    Before or alongside each introduction, your advisor provides the membership director with a prepared profile of you. This gives the director the context they need to evaluate your candidacy before your first direct conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Applications, Interviews, and Visits Supported">
    As clubs express interest, your advisor guides you through each club's specific requirements: formal application forms, letters of recommendation, candidate interviews, and site visits. No step is navigated alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Acceptance and Credit">
    When you receive and accept a membership offer, your $1,500 deposit is credited against your initiation fee at that club. Your advisor confirms the credit with the club's business office to ensure a seamless transition.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who the Concierge Service Is For

The Concierge service is particularly valuable if any of the following describes your situation:

- You are targeting **by-invitation or highly competitive clubs** where cold applications are ignored
- You are **new to private club membership** and uncertain how to present yourself or navigate the process
- You want access to clubs with **implicit or undisclosed waitlists** that are not well-publicized
- You are **relocating** and need fast, expert guidance on clubs in an unfamiliar market
- You are pursuing **multiple clubs simultaneously** and want a single professional managing the process
- Your **schedule does not allow** for the time-intensive back-and-forth of self-managed applications

<Warning>
  The Concierge service does not guarantee acceptance at any specific club — no service can, because final membership decisions rest with the club's board. What it guarantees is expert representation and a full refund if no placement is secured within 90 days.
</Warning>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What counts as a 'placement' for the 90-day guarantee?">
    A placement is a formal membership offer from a club that your advisor introduced you to through the Concierge process. If no such offer is extended within 90 days, you receive a full $1,500 refund.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use the Concierge service if I have already applied to clubs on my own?">
    Yes. Your advisor will review any pending applications and can take over management of those relationships in addition to pursuing new introductions on your behalf.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is the $1,500 credit applied to my initiation fee?">
    Upon acceptance, your advisor confirms the credit arrangement with the club's membership office. The $1,500 is deducted from the initiation fee you pay directly to the club. The credit is between you and the club, facilitated by TPC.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use the Concierge service for club types other than golf?">
    Yes. The service covers all club types in the directory — country clubs, yacht clubs, city clubs, athletic clubs, and more. Your advisor's network spans the full range of private membership categories.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I want to join more than one club?">
    The Concierge engagement covers your placement at one club. If you want representation for a second club (common when seeking both a golf club and a city club), contact the Concierge team to discuss a multi-club engagement.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
