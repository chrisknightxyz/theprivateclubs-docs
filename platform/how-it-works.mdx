The Private Clubs is the largest private club directory in North America, with 51,633+ clubs spanning golf, country, yacht, city, athletic, and dozens of other membership categories. Whether you are a first-time applicant exploring options or a seasoned member relocating to a new city, the platform gives you the research tools, transparent fee data, and human support you need to find and join the right club — on your timeline.

## The Member Journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse the Directory">
    Start at [theprivateclubs.com/clubs](/clubs) and explore clubs by state, type, or name. Every listing shows the club's initiation fee, annual dues, waitlist status, amenities, and Google rating — data points that most clubs keep hidden and that no other directory publishes at this scale.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter and Shortlist">
    Narrow your search using filters for club type, initiation fee range, annual dues range, and membership availability (open, waitlist, or by-invitation). Save clubs to your shortlist as you browse to keep track of the ones that stand out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare Side by Side">
    Add up to four clubs to the comparison tool to evaluate initiation fees, annual dues, amenities, founding year, course architect (for golf clubs), and member ratings on the same screen. Fees display in USD by default and can be viewed in 49 currencies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply or Engage the Concierge">
    Submit a membership inquiry directly through the club's listing page — your application routes straight to the club's membership director. If you prefer a guided, hands-on experience, engage the [Membership Concierge service](/members/concierge-service) and receive a dedicated advisor, warm introductions, and full support through acceptance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive a Response">
    The membership director reviews your application and responds directly. For open clubs this can take days; waitlist and by-invitation clubs may have longer timelines. Your Concierge advisor manages all follow-up communication if you chose that path.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept and Join">
    Once accepted, complete any club-specific onboarding steps. If you used the Concierge service, your $1,500 refundable deposit is credited at acceptance — effectively making the service free upon successful placement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Makes the Platform Different

The Private Clubs is the only directory that publishes initiation fees and annual dues at scale. Most clubs do not advertise this information publicly, which means prospective members historically had to cold-call clubs or rely on word of mouth just to understand the cost of entry. The platform removes that friction so you can research, compare, and shortlist clubs the same way you would any other major financial decision.

<Note>
  Fees are sourced directly from clubs and updated regularly. If you notice an outdated figure on a listing, use the "Suggest an Edit" link on that club's page.
</Note>

## For Clubs: Claiming Your Listing

Membership directors and club managers can claim their listing to take control of their club's profile. A claimed listing lets you:

- Update fees, amenities, photos, and membership availability
- Receive qualified leads directly from prospective members
- Respond to inquiries through the platform's messaging system
- Promote open membership periods or waitlist openings

<Tip>
  Claimed and verified listings rank higher in search results and display a verified badge that increases applicant confidence.
</Tip>
