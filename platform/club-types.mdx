The Private Clubs directory spans more than a dozen distinct membership club categories, covering 51,633+ clubs across all 50 states. Whether you are searching for a competitive golf program, a social dining community, or a private hangar for your aircraft, the directory organizes every listing by club type so you can filter immediately to the category that fits your lifestyle.

Use the URL pattern `/clubs/type/[type-slug]` to browse any category directly — for example, `/clubs/type/golf` lists every golf club in the directory.

## All Supported Club Types

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Golf" icon="golf-ball-tee">
    **Slug:** `golf`

    Golf clubs center on access to a private course, practice facilities, and a competitive or recreational playing community. Memberships typically include unlimited rounds, preferred tee times, locker facilities, and a clubhouse — with initiation fees that range from a few thousand dollars to well over $100,000 at elite clubs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Country Club" icon="house-flag">
    **Slug:** `country_club`

    Country clubs combine golf with a broader suite of amenities — tennis courts, swimming pools, fine dining, banquet facilities, and family programming. They function as a social hub for the surrounding community and often represent the most comprehensive private membership experience available.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Yacht & Sailing" icon="sailboat">
    **Slug:** `yacht`

    Yacht clubs provide private marina access, mooring and dry-storage facilities, racing programs, and a community of fellow boaters. Many offer reciprocal cruising privileges at partner clubs along the coast or on inland waterways, making them valuable for members who travel by water.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="City Club" icon="building-columns">
    **Slug:** `city`

    City clubs are urban private clubs typically housed in a downtown building, offering members a refined setting for business lunches, networking events, private dining rooms, and overnight accommodations. They serve professionals who want a home base in the city without the overhead of a private office.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Athletic Club" icon="dumbbell">
    **Slug:** `athletic`

    Athletic clubs provide premium fitness facilities — including pools, racquet courts, group fitness studios, personal training, and spa services — in a private, members-only environment. They differ from commercial gyms in their exclusivity, service standards, and social programming.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Aviation Club" icon="plane">
    **Slug:** `aviation`

    Aviation clubs offer private hangar space, fuel purchasing programs, runway access, and a community of fellow pilots and aircraft owners. Some clubs extend to flight simulation, safety training, and organized fly-in events at destinations across the country.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Equestrian Club" icon="horse">
    **Slug:** `equestrian`

    Equestrian clubs provide stabling, riding arenas, trails, training programs, and competition facilities for horse owners and riders. Membership typically covers board for one or more horses, access to the grounds, and participation in club-organized shows and clinics.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Social Club" icon="champagne-glasses">
    **Slug:** `social`

    Social clubs focus on community, events, and shared experiences rather than a single sport or amenity. Members enjoy curated programming — dinners, cultural outings, speaker series, and private parties — in an environment designed around connection and belonging.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Tennis Club" icon="table-tennis-paddle-ball">
    **Slug:** `tennis`

    Tennis clubs provide dedicated court access, professional instruction, leagues, and competitive ladders in a private setting. Many offer both indoor and outdoor courts, ensuring year-round play, along with social events built around the game.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Automotive Club" icon="car">
    **Slug:** `automotive`

    Automotive clubs cater to car enthusiasts with private garage space, track days, concours events, rally tours, and a community of fellow collectors. Some facilities include climate-controlled storage, detailing services, and workshop space for members to work on their vehicles.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Ski Club" icon="person-skiing">
    **Slug:** `ski`

    Ski clubs offer private mountain access, ski-in/ski-out lodging, equipment storage, race programs, and on-mountain dining privileges. Membership removes the friction of public resort lines and provides a consistent, high-service environment across the ski season.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Fishing Club" icon="fish">
    **Slug:** `fishing`

    Fishing clubs protect exclusive access to private water — rivers, lakes, ponds, or coastal areas — stocked and managed for fly fishing, freshwater, or saltwater angling. They often include guides, equipment storage, and rustic lodge accommodations on the property.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Beach Club" icon="umbrella-beach">
    **Slug:** `beach`

    Beach clubs grant private access to oceanfront or lakefront property, including reserved beach areas, cabanas, watercraft storage, pools, and dining. They are particularly sought after in coastal markets where public beach access is limited or congested.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Hunting Club" icon="crosshairs">
    **Slug:** `hunting`

    Hunting clubs provide exclusive access to managed land for upland bird, waterfowl, or big-game hunting, along with guides, dog kennels, lodging, and game processing. Land management practices at private clubs typically produce far superior habitat and populations compared to public hunting grounds.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Wellness Club" icon="spa">
    **Slug:** `wellness`

    Wellness clubs are premium, members-only destinations focused on health optimization — offering services such as cryotherapy, IV therapy, infrared saunas, meditation studios, nutritional consulting, and integrative medicine alongside traditional spa treatments.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Arts Club" icon="palette">
    **Slug:** `arts`

    Arts clubs bring together members with shared interests in visual art, performing arts, literature, or music, providing studio space, galleries, performance venues, and a calendar of curated cultural programming. They often have storied histories as gathering places for creative communities.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Adventure Club" icon="mountain">
    **Slug:** `adventure`

    Adventure clubs organize members around outdoor pursuits — hiking, climbing, kayaking, cycling, or multi-sport expeditions. Membership provides access to guided trips, equipment lending libraries, group expeditions, and a vetted community of fellow adventurers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Dining Club" icon="utensils">
    **Slug:** `dining`

    Dining clubs center on exceptional private culinary experiences, including reserved dining rooms, chef's table events, wine programs, and exclusive tasting menus unavailable to the public. They suit members who prioritize hospitality and gastronomy as their primary membership benefit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Residential Club" icon="house">
    **Slug:** `residential`

    Residential clubs combine real estate ownership or rental with access to shared private amenities — clubhouses, pools, sports courts, concierge services, and organized social programming. Membership is often tied to property ownership within a planned community or development.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Business Club" icon="briefcase">
    **Slug:** `business`

    Business clubs serve professionals and executives with private meeting rooms, workspace, video-conferencing suites, and a curated member network built for deal-making and career development. Think of them as the private-club equivalent of a premium co-working environment.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Browse by Type

Navigate directly to any club type using the directory URL pattern:

```
https://theprivateclubs.com/clubs/type/[type-slug]
```

For example:
- `/clubs/type/golf` — all golf clubs
- `/clubs/type/yacht` — all yacht and sailing clubs
- `/clubs/type/automotive` — all automotive clubs

You can also combine a type filter with a state filter to narrow results geographically. See the [Searching Clubs guide](/members/searching-clubs) for the full set of filtering options.
