## How Verification Works

Every member community on The Private Clubs is restricted to verified members — no lurkers, no outsiders. When you subscribe, you complete a one-time verification so other members know you're the real deal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subscribe to a Plan">
    Choose Your Club or All Clubs and complete checkout. Your account is created immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Your Verification">
    Provide any one of the following:
    - Your member number
    - A photo of your membership card
    - Direct confirmation from your club's membership office
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Verified">
    The Private Clubs reviews your submission and grants community access within **24–48 hours**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the Conversation">
    Post questions, share insights, and connect with fellow members inside your verified community.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Verification is a one-time step per club. If you subscribe to All Clubs, you can access any club's community once you've verified membership at that club. You don't need to re-verify when you renew.
</Note>

## Privacy Protections

Your activity inside a club community is only visible to other verified members of that same club. The Private Clubs does not share your identity, membership details, or discussion history with third parties, advertisers, or other clubs. Your member number and card photo are used solely for verification and are not stored beyond that process.
