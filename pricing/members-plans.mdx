The Private Clubs does not sell plans to club members.

Membership fees, dues, initiation fees, and any other charges are set by each private club and paid directly to that club. The Private Clubs does not collect or process those payments on a club's behalf.

For current pricing, application requirements, and payment terms, contact the club directly.
