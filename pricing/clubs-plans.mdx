The Private Clubs offers a single, straightforward plan for clubs and membership directors: the **Founding Club** plan. It covers everything you need to establish a verified presence on the directory, start receiving pre-qualified membership enquiries, and connect with other directors across the country — all at a price that's locked in for life for the first 100 clubs to join.

## Founding Club Plan

There are two charges when you join as a Founding Club. Both run on the same card at checkout at the same time — there's no separate setup flow.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="One-Time Setup" icon="rocket">
    **$500 — charged once at signup**

    Covers everything needed to get your club live on the directory within 24 hours:

    - Club verification
    - Profile production
    - AI directory placement (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and more)
    - Founding Club badge on your listing
  </Card>
  <Card title="Monthly Membership" icon="calendar">
    **$99/month — billed annually**

    - Pre-qualified membership enquiries delivered to your inbox
    - Analytics dashboard (views, impressions, enquiries, fee benchmarks)
    - Access to the 2,400+ member membership director community
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  The $500 setup fee and the $99/mo subscription are **separate charges** that run simultaneously at checkout on the same card. Your annual subscription ($1,188) and the setup fee ($500) are billed at the same time when you sign up.
</Note>

## Founding Club Pricing

<Warning>
  Founding Club pricing is limited to the **first 100 clubs**. Once that cap is reached, the one-time setup fee and monthly rate will increase. If you're reading this, spots are still available — but act before the cohort closes.
</Warning>

When you join as a Founding Club, your $99/mo rate is locked in for life. If The Private Clubs raises prices for new clubs in the future, your rate never changes. There is no long-term contract: you can cancel your monthly subscription at any time without penalty, and you keep the verified profile and Founding Club badge you earned through the one-time setup.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does it cost?">
    The Founding Club plan has two components charged at the same time on the same card at checkout:

    - **$500 one-time setup fee** — covers verification, profile production, AI directory placement, and your Founding Club badge
    - **$99/month billed annually** ($1,188/year) — covers pre-qualified enquiries, the analytics dashboard, and membership director community access

    Both charges are initiated simultaneously when you complete checkout. There are no hidden fees.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the pricing locked in?">
    Yes. Founding Club pricing is locked for life for the first 100 clubs. Your $99/mo rate will never increase as long as you maintain your subscription, even if The Private Clubs raises prices for clubs that join after the Founding Club cohort closes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel?">
    Yes. There is no long-term contract. You can cancel your $99/mo subscription at any time — your access continues until the end of the current billing period. The $500 one-time setup fee is non-refundable, but your verified profile and Founding Club badge remain active while your subscription is live.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How quickly will my profile be live?">
    Within **24 hours** of completing verification. Once you check out, the team begins verification and profile production immediately. Most clubs are live on the directory — and indexed for AI discovery — within the same business day.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's included in the $500 setup fee?">
    The $500 covers four things:

    1. **Verification** — confirms your club's identity and membership credentials
    2. **Profile production** — builds out your full listing with accurate data, amenities, and fee information
    3. **AI directory placement** — indexes your club for discovery via ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and other AI tools
    4. **Founding Club badge** — displays on your listing to signal verified, active participation

    This is a one-time charge. You will never be asked to pay the setup fee again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's included in the $99/mo subscription?">
    Your monthly subscription covers three ongoing benefits:

    1. **Pre-qualified enquiries** — membership leads filtered by location, club type, and budget, delivered via email, SMS, WhatsApp, or CRM integration
    2. **Analytics dashboard** — real-time data on profile views, impressions, enquiry trends, and fee benchmarking against comparable clubs
    3. **Membership director community** — private access to a network of 2,400+ directors from clubs across North America

    These benefits are active as long as your subscription is running.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are both charges on the same card?">
    Yes. The $500 one-time setup fee and the $99/mo annual subscription ($1,188) are both charged to the same payment card at checkout simultaneously. There is no separate billing flow or invoice for either charge.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
