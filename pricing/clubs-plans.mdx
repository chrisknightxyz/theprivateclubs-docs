All club plans are priced in **USD** and include Network access for pre-qualified membership enquiries, a lead inbox, and team seats. Plus is the entry point for a single club. Pro adds Community access and unlimited listings for club groups. Elite adds website and SEO/AEO services. Max is the full-stack option.

## Billing and Cancellation

Your selected billing interval determines your billing period. Monthly plans renew each month, quarterly plans renew each quarter, and yearly plans renew each year. You can cancel before the next renewal to prevent the next charge. Access continues through the end of the billing period already paid.
